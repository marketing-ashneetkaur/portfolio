001 / About

About Ashneet.

Vancouver

I am a fourth year BBA candidate at Capilano University, concentrating in Marketing and Business Information Systems. Originally from India, now based in Vancouver, I have spent the last six years working at the intersection of brand, content, and customer experience.

My background spans social media management, community outreach, event programming, and visual content creation. At Zen Maker Lab I led social campaigns and grew the client base through proactive engagement. At the Capilano Students' Union I coordinated communications and outreach across a wide student community. Today I work as a Sales Specialist at Apple, where client relations and brand standards are part of every shift.

I work best in collaborative, fast moving teams where ideas can be tested quickly and where the work has a clear audience in mind.
